# Gameshow night

The Chihuahua Cup: a family game show you run from a laptop on the TV. Everything is in one HTML file, and it works with no internet.

## Play it

1. Download this repository (green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**) and unzip it.
2. Double-click the launcher:
   - Windows: `Play The Chihuahua Cup (Windows).bat`
   - Mac: `Play The Chihuahua Cup (Mac).command` (the first time, right-click it, choose Open, then Open again)
3. Connect the laptop to the TV, press **Start the show**, then **Full screen**.

If the launcher doesn't work, open `chihuahua-cup.html` in Edge, Chrome or Brave. It plays the same.

## What's in it

- **Rounds:** Dingbats, Music Round, Blind Taste Test, Closest Wins, Who in This Family?, Minute to Win It and The Final Chase, plus optional Rare Answers, True or False, Emoji Films, Anagrams, Missing Vowels, Letters and Numbers, Bid to Name, The Price Is Right and Zoomed In.
- **Scoreboard:** 2 to 8 players, number keys to score, undo, and score corrections. After each round the lowest scorer takes a jelly bean.
- **Question bank:** about 600 Final Chase questions in twelve topics, each with an easy, medium or hard rating that sets its points. The answers and ratings were checked by independent reviewers.
- **Your own questions:** paste them into any round, with a difficulty if you want one.
- **Answers for the host:** a host screen that follows the show on a second display, or on a phone via a QR code.

The keyboard shortcuts are listed in Setup. `READ ME FIRST.txt` has the full instructions for the night.
