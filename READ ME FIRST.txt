THE CHIHUAHUA CUP
=================

Everything the game needs is in this folder. It does not use the internet,
an account, or Claude.

TO PLAY
  Windows:  double-click  "Play The Chihuahua Cup (Windows)"
  Mac:      double-click  "Play The Chihuahua Cup (Mac)"
            (first time only: right-click it, choose Open, then Open again)

The game opens in its own window, with no address bar or tabs. Press
"Full screen" in the top corner once it is on the TV.

WHAT IS IN THE FOLDER
  chihuahua-cup.html   the game itself: all rounds, questions and scoring
  Play ... (Windows)   launcher for Windows
  Play ... (Mac)       launcher for Mac
  data                 appears after the first run: your scores, settings,
                       photos and edited questions are kept here

SEEING THE ANSWERS WHILE YOU HOST
  On the laptop: in Setup press "Open the host screen". It lists every
  question and answer and follows the show by itself. Press Windows key + P
  and choose Extend, put the show window on the TV and keep the host screen
  on the laptop.
  On a phone: copy chihuahua-cup.html onto the phone, open it and press
  "Open the host screen". On the laptop, in Setup, press "Show the code for
  your phone", scan it with the phone camera, copy the text and paste it
  into the phone's host screen. No internet is needed.

MOVING IT TO ANOTHER COMPUTER
  Copy the whole folder, including "data", to a USB stick or the other
  computer. Scores, settings and photos come with it.

STARTING COMPLETELY FRESH
  Use "Reset for a new game" in Setup to zero the scores, or delete the
  "data" folder to wipe scores, settings and photos.

IF THE LAUNCHER DOES NOT WORK
  Double-click chihuahua-cup.html. It opens in your normal browser and
  plays exactly the same. (Scores saved that way are kept by that browser,
  separately from the "data" folder.)

WHAT THE LAUNCHER NEEDS
  Microsoft Edge (already on every Windows 10 and 11 PC), Google Chrome or
  Brave. It uses that browser's engine to draw the window, offline.
